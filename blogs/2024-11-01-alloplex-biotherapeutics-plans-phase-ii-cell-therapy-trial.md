Alloplex Biotherapeutics on Thursday said it will submit an investigational new drug application to the US Food and Drug Administration to begin a Phase II trial of its autologous peripheral blood mononuclear cell (PBMC)-derived cellular therapy, Suplexa, with immune checkpoint inhibitors in certain colorectal cancers.
