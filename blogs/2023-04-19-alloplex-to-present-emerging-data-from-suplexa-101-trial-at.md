We are pleased to announce that Dr. Frank Borriello, Founder and CEO of Alloplex Biotherapeutics, will be presenting at the upcoming World Oncology Cell Therapy Congress, being held in Boston, MA, on April 25 & 26, 2023. The presentation will include emerging data from the SUPLEXA-101 clinical trial and highlight the development of an unprecedented blood-based pharmacodynamic signature of SUPLEXA activity.
